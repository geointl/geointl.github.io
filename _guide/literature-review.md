## Overview

The goal of a literature review is to find the status quo and define the research gap: what is already done, what is known, and what is still missing.

A typical workflow combines four tools:

1. **Find** papers with [Google Scholar](#google-scholar).
2. **Map** the field around key papers with [Connected Papers](#connected-papers).
3. **Understand** key papers in depth with [alphaXiv](#alphaxiv).
4. **Manage** references and citations throughout with [Zotero](#zotero).

Each tool fits a different stage, so use them together rather than picking one.

## Google Scholar

[scholar.google.com](https://scholar.google.com)

- **When:** Every stage — initial keyword search, checking a paper's impact, and staying up to date.
- **How:** Search with keywords, select a paper, then use **Cited by** to browse that is the prvious work and newer work. Save papers to **My library**, export BibTeX via the cite (") button, and set up **alerts** for keywords or key authors.
- **Why:** The broadest coverage (journals, conferences, theses, preprints) and citation counts that quickly show which papers matter.

## Connected Papers

[connectedpapers.com](https://www.connectedpapers.com)

- **When:** Early stage — exploring a new topic once you have one or two key papers.
- **How:** Enter a seed paper to get a graph of similar papers. Use **Prior works** to find the foundational papers and **Derivative works** to find recent follow-ups.
- **Why:** Shows the structure of a field at a glance and surfaces relevant papers that keyword search misses, since similarity is based on shared citations rather than keywords.

## alphaXiv

[alphaxiv.org](https://www.alphaxiv.org)

- **When:** Deep-reading stage — once you have picked the key papers you need to understand in detail.
- **How:** Replace `arxiv` with `alphaxiv` in any arXiv URL to open the paper. It works for any arXiv paper, recent or years old. Ask the built-in AI assistant about the methods, equations, or results, and read the community comments alongside the paper.
- **Why:** A free AI assistant that answers questions in the context of the paper you are reading, so you can work through a dense paper in depth without a paid tool.

> [!NOTE]
> alphaXiv is not only for early-stage preprints. Many established and highly cited papers are also on arXiv, so any paper with an arXiv version can be investigated very efficiently with AI on alphaXiv.

## Zotero

[zotero.org](https://www.zotero.org)

- **When:** Every stage — from the first search to writing the manuscript.
- **How:** Install the desktop app and the **Zotero Connector** browser extension, then save papers with one click from Google Scholar, publisher pages, or arXiv. Organize them into collections and tags, and read and annotate PDFs in the built-in reader. When writing, insert citations with the Word or Google Docs plugin, or export BibTeX for LaTeX.
- **Why:** Free and open source. It keeps all your references and notes in one place and formats citations and bibliographies automatically in any citation style.
