Openings

We are actively recruiting motivated researchers to join GeoIntelligence at UMass Amherst. If you are excited about pushing the boundaries of computational geotechnics with AI, we’d love to hear from you. Please see our research page for more about our research.

PhD Positions

We are looking for PhD students with strong backgrounds in civil/geotechnical engineering, computational mechanics, or machine learning. Experience with numerical methods or scientific computing is a plus.

Postdoc Positions
